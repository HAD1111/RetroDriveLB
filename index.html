Childhood Genesis

It started with a movie.

Long before the sound of ratchets and the scent of warmed motor oil filled the garage, there was a child sitting cross-legged in a Beirut living room, watching classic black-and-white films on Egyptian television beside his mother.

While the actors delivered their legendary dialogues, a young Charbel was captivated by what carried them: the graceful sweeping fenders, chrome grilles, and unmistakable presence of vintage automobiles gliding across the screen.

“The movies ended, but the cars stayed with him.”
The Evolution

Then the dream became real.

Childhood curiosity did not fade; it quietly waited. In 2005, following a major career transition, Charbel Maroun Mehawej turned his enduring fascination into a devoted life’s work.

2005

A New Chapter

Stepping away from conventional career tracks to dedicate hands, heart, and time to vintage mechanical preservation.

Restoration Milestone

One car changed everything.

1970 Oldsmobile Cutlass

Found dormant under decades of dust with seized pistons and tired paint. Charbel and his friend Rabih dismantled the car piece by piece, nut by nut, and rebuilt it with unwavering dedication over 18 months of late-night garage sessions.

“Piece by piece. Bolt by bolt. Until it came back to life.”

— Charbel & Rabih, Restoration Journal
01

Teardown & Cataloging

Every individual trim clip, bolt, and chrome script was unbolted, marked, and sorted into wooden trays.

02

Chassis & Metal Straightening

Bare frame stripped of surface rust. Hammer-formed sheet metal fabrication to restore original body panel tolerances.

03

V8 Rocket 350 Blueprinting

Crankshaft balanced, new bearings seated, camshaft dialed in, and the 4-barrel carburetor tuned for Lebanese sea-level air.

04

Deep Lacquer & Final Reassembly

Seven coats of hand-rubbed metallic finish and re-chromed bumpers. The day the ignition turned over, the workshop shook.

Our Ethos

You don’t just own a classic.
You take care of it.

For Charbel, vintage cars are not static investment assets parked in museum isolation. They are organic mechanical creatures that breathe, expand, and need regular tactile care.

Constant Attention & Tuning

Listening to valve clearances, checking points gaps, and synchronizing twin carbs by ear rather than relying on computer codes.

Preservation of Mechanical Character

Never erasing the soul. Respecting factory spot welds, preserving original gauges, and maintaining authentic driving ergonomics.

Driven With Purpose

A classic car that does not run becomes brittle. Taking them through mountain roads keeps gaskets lubricated and histories alive.

“Every time you clean a chrome bezel or check the oil level, you are conversing with the engineers who built it half a century ago.”

The Atelier

From passion to craft.

What began as two friends in a personal garage has expanded into an authentic preservation workshop for Lebanon's finest vintage machines.

CRAFT 01

Classic Car Restoration

Comprehensive nut-and-bolt, body-off restorations. From rust repair and metal shaping to complete wiring harness reconstruction, factory-correct upholstery, and mirror-finish paint.

Chassis Alignment Bare Metal Preparation Factory Color Codes
CRAFT 02

Preservation & Maintenance

Dedicated care schedules for running classics. Carburetor balancing, valve lash adjustment, vintage electrical sorting, and pre-cruise safety inspections.

Carb Synchronization Fluids & Lubrication Brake Overhauls
CRAFT 03

Mechanical Restoration

Engine rebuilds (American V8s, German Inline-6s, Italian Twin-Cams), manual gearboxes, overdrives, differentials, and cooling upgrades for the Lebanese summer climate.

Engine Blueprinting Head Porting High-Flow Cooling
CRAFT 04

Custom Builds & Period Mod

Gentleman rally preparations, period-correct suspension dampening, stainless exhaust fabrication, and subtle drivability enhancements that preserve vintage character.

Rally Setups Exhaust Fabrication Uprated Bushings
CRAFT 05

Classic Car Sourcing

Locating authentic unmolested survivors tucked away in Lebanese villages and regional barns. Rigorous pre-purchase inspection and provenance verification.

Barn Find Hunting Provenance Auditing Import & Clearing

The Brotherhood

More than a garage.

“A place for people who still get excited when they hear an old engine start.”

In a world rushing toward soulless screens and silent electric pods, Retro Drive LB is an anchor for those who value mechanical honesty. Here, conversations happen over open engine bays, warm Turkish coffee, and maps marked with the best back roads of Lebanon.

“You come here with a loose manifold and leave with three new friends, grease on your sleeves, and a route to the mountains you never knew existed.”
